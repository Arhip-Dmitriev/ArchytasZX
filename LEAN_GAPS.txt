Cases the engine can produce that have no Lean proof path

1. Dimension binder and splitter (B, S) under the normaliser.
The binder's entry is [k = a*t + b] with a < s, b < t, k < s*t, where t is a dimension symbol.
The normaliser's index polynomials have integer coefficients, so the delta needs t as a
coefficient. Eliminating it needs div/mod terms (k div t, k mod t). An X spider or Fourier box on
an s*t wire also needs characters omega_s^(t*k1*l1), whose sums depend on gcd(t, s), and gcd is
not determined by symbolic monomials. Verdicts reached by rewriting are proved by the connective
lemmas (Connective.lean). Verdicts that need the normaliser on B or S nodes are not.

2. Evaluated quadratic Gauss sums.
The symbolic simplifier closes sum_{k<d} omega_d^(a*k^2 + b*k + c) by the classical formula
sqrt(d)(1+i)(1+i^(-d))/2, including Gauss's sign, and by Landsberg-Schaar reciprocity for |a| >= 2.
Neither theorem is in Mathlib, which has finite-field Gauss sums without the sign and the Jacobi
theta transformation. No formalization was found in Lean, Isabelle, Coq or HOL. Quadratic phases
are proved only when their sums cancel between the two sides.

3. Phase rules with a symbolic coefficient (for example theta*k).
At the spider's bound index s the rule contributes ex(theta)^s, an atom raised to a summed
variable. Summing it gives a geometric series (ex(theta)^D - 1)/(ex(theta) - 1). That is a
rational function of the atom, outside the normaliser's monomial coefficients.

4. Scalars depending on a multiplicity non-geometrically, with port boxes.
For node boxes, eval X(n) = x0*t0^n, so a non-geometric ratio between the two sides' scalars
forces both networks to vanish. Port-box families have no such product form, and their induction
step needs the two sides' scalars to grow by the same constant factor per copy.

5. Scalars outside the exact model whose ratio is outside it too.
Opaque scalars (pi, roots of non-monomials, and the like) are handled when the two sides' scalars
differ by an exact factor. Otherwise the verdict asserts an identity between transcendental
constants. Deciding such identities is undecidable in general (Richardson's theorem).

6. Node boxes meeting the rest of the diagram at a wire, and port boxes inside port boxes, under
the normaliser.
The symbolic contraction declines both, so no symbolic-contraction verdict contains them. Verdicts
reached by rewriting use the lifted rule lemmas. A family whose copies share a wire with the rest
does not split into a peeled copy beside the rest, which the normaliser's induction step needs.

7. Deferred dimension constraints in families.
Outside bang boxes a rewrite proof that assumed two dimensions equal states the equalities as
hypotheses: the proof over the merged network is transported to the networks at their own
dimensions (Equal.of_mapDim'). The transport maps plain dimension indices only. A family's bundle
indices are derived from them, and the transport does not map those, so such a family has no
export with the constraint as a hypothesis.

8. Nodes whose legs differ in dimension, in families.
A spider whose legs have dimensions such as d^p and d^q is proved under the hypothesis
d^p = d^q, but only outside bang boxes. Inside a family the positivity of every dimension value
is stated over the family's natural-number parameters, and the exponent symbols carry no
positivity hypothesis.
