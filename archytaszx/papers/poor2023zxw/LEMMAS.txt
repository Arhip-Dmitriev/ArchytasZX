LEMMAS OF [P1]
==============

paper.pdf and src/ below are in references/poor2023zxw/ (downloaded, not tracked).

Source: [P1], Appendix A.1 "Lemmas and Proofs" (PDF pages 23-35), src/lemmas.tex. Lemmas 1-6
and 48 belong to the completeness proof (normal forms, PDF pages 35-44); they state rewriting
procedures, not single diagram equations, and are listed at the end.

Each entry: number (as printed), LaTeX label, statement figure (src/figures/), side
conditions as stated, proof figure, and the step labels of the proof as the PDF prints them
above its equality signs: an axiom name, a notation's definition (HZ, Du, Mu, WN, YT, S3, S4,
H dagger), or the number of an earlier lemma. "encoded" names the engine command file holding
the lemma at a fixed dimension, run by the corpus run (docs/COMPLETENESS.txt, section 9).

Lemma 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 48 (completeness proof)
    Lemma 1: any normal form can be rewritten into its unique form. Lemmas 2-6: the Z box,
    the W node, the Hadamard box, the partial trace and the tensor product of normal forms
    rewrite into normal form. Lemma 48: used in the proof of Lemma 4. Their proofs are
    rewriting procedures over arbitrary diagrams; they are the argument of the completeness
    theorem itself, and are not encoded as single equations.
