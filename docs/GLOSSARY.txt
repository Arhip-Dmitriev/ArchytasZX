ARCHYTASZX DOCUMENTATION
========================

Glossary

Applies to: ArchytasZX 2.0.x


abstraction
    Replacing a concrete number typed by the user with a fresh symbol, recording the number in
    the parameter environment. Dim.abstract, Mult.abstract.

assumption
    A dimension equality a derivation relied on without verifying it (a deferred dimension
    constraint). Reported with every verdict and certificate that depends on it, and stated as
    a hypothesis of an exported Lean theorem.

bang box
    A scope in a diagram standing for a symbolic number of repetitions. A node-scope box
    repeats whole nodes; a port-scope box repeats legs of one node. DIAGRAM.txt, section 4.

boundary
    The ordered lists of a diagram's open ports: boundary inputs and boundary outputs.

certificate
    A replayable record of a derivation, with the method by which its claimed equality is
    checked. PROOFS.txt, section 5.

character sum
    The identity sum_{k=0}^{d-1} omega_d^{j k} = d [j == 0 mod d], central to symbolic
    contraction and to the Lean normaliser.

comparison view
    A copy of a diagram with spider legs in canonical order, an empty parameter environment
    and a simplified scalar; two diagrams are identified when their views are isomorphic.

copy-major
    The layout of an instantiated family: copy 0 of every slot, then copy 1, and so on.

deferred
    Said of a dimension equation the unifier could not decide, and of a validation issue or
    match condition resting on one. Deferred items never fail validation; they become
    assumptions.

derivation
    A sequence of rewrite steps from an initial to a final diagram.

dimension
    The number of levels of a qudit; a Dim: a positive integer, a symbol, or a product or power
    of these. Stored on every port.

e-graph
    A data structure holding many equivalent diagrams at once, partitioned into equivalence
    classes; used for equality saturation. REWRITE.txt, section 7.

effect
    A spider with one input and no output.

family
    A diagram containing bang boxes, standing for one diagram per value of its multiplicities.

generator
    A node type: Z and X spiders, the Fourier box F, the triangle T and its inverse Ti, the W
    node, the binder B and the splitter S.

induction
    Proof of an equality between families for every value of a multiplicity, from a base case
    and a step case. PROOFS.txt, section 4.

kernel
    The small set of inference rules from which theorems about diagrams are built.
    PROOFS.txt, section 6.

match
    One occurrence of a rule's left-hand side in a diagram, with the outcome of each side
    condition.

multiplicity
    The number of repetitions a bang box stands for; a Mult: a non-negative integer, a symbol,
    or sums and products of these.

normal form
    The fixpoint of the normal-form rule set; agreement of normal forms proves equality.

oracle
    Numeric contraction of small concrete instances, used to find counterexamples and to
    cross-check derivations. Evidence, never proof.

parameter environment
    The map from a diagram's symbols to the concrete values the user supplied for them.

phase
    An angle in turns; a phase vector gives one phase per level of a spider, index 0 fixed to
    zero.

qufinite ZX(W)-calculus
    The graphical calculus of qudits of arbitrary and mixed finite dimensions, with Z and X
    spiders, the Fourier box, the triangle, the W node and the dimension connectives.

rule
    A rewrite: a pattern, a builder, side conditions and the exact scalar introduced.
    RULES.txt.

scalable notation
    The sheet-wire reading of bang-boxed diagrams, in which a scale stands for k copies or a
    sheet of k wires. DIAGRAM.txt, section 6.

side condition
    A named condition a match must satisfy for a rule to fire.

soundness
    The property that every rewrite preserves the denoted linear map, scalar included, and
    every EQUAL or UNEQUAL verdict is correct.

spider
    A Z or X generator; any number of legs, a phase vector.

state
    A spider or diagram with no input.

symbolic contraction
    Contraction of a diagram with every dimension, phase and multiplicity kept as a symbol.
    SEMANTICS.txt, section 5.

tactic
    A program that maps a diagram to a stream of derivations. REWRITE.txt, section 8.

turn
    The unit of phase: t turns is the angle 2*pi*t.

UNKNOWN
    The verdict of a decision procedure that could neither prove nor refute an equality.
