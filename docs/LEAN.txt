ARCHYTASZX DOCUMENTATION
========================

The Lean 4 Library and Proof Export

Applies to: ArchytasZX 0.19.x
Lean library: archytaszx/lean (Lean 4.35.0, Mathlib)
Exporters: archytaszx.semantics.kernel (lean), lean_net, lean_sym, lean_refute, lean_numeric,
           lean_scalar, lean_lang (with its mirror lean_lsym)


1. PURPOSE
----------

The engine's proofs and refutations can be exported as Lean 4 source and checked by Lean,
independently of the engine. The export targets a Lean library, bundled with the package, in
which a diagram is a tensor network over the complex numbers and every rewrite rule is a lemma
proved once. A proof that Lean accepts is therefore correct relative to Lean, Mathlib, the
library's definitions, and the translation of the two diagrams into networks.


2. SETTING UP
-------------

See INSTALL.txt, section 3. In brief, from the library directory:

    lake exe cache get
    lake build
    lake env lean /path/to/proof.lean

From Python, archytaszx.semantics.lean_net.check(source, path) writes the source to path and
runs Lean on it. In the shell:

    :certificate lean proof.lean        write the stored proof to proof.lean
    :certificate lean                   print it instead


3. THE SEMANTICS IN LEAN
------------------------

3.1 Networks (ArchytasZX.Net)

A Net lists generator instances whose legs carry wire labels. A label shared by two legs is a
contracted index; int lists the labels summed over and bnd the open boundary labels. eval is
the sum over the internal labels of the product of the generator tensors. An environment Env
gives the dimension of every label, the dimensions of the nodes and their phase vectors.

The main statement is

    Equal E s N s' N'           s * eval N = s' * eval N'

between two scaled networks under one environment: the same tensor, scalars included.

3.2 Library modules

  Module       Content
  -----------  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Net          networks over ℂ and their evaluation
  Iso          isoOK, a decidable check that two networks agree up to renaming of summed
               labels, node order and spider leg order; iso proves it sound
  Rules        every rule of the engine as a local equation (LocalEq) between its two sides,
               for every leg labelling the engine produces, and wire_elim for bare wires
  Closed       the character sum, the phaseless X spider as a scaled delta modulo d, and the
               Fourier box squared
  ZXRules      cap, Fourier colour change, state copy, triangle and Fourier cancellation
  Bialgebra    the bialgebra law
  Hopf         the Hopf law
  WRules       W and triangle rules
  WZ           the Z-into-W bialgebra
  Connective   binder and splitter rules
  Bundle       families: a bundle label of base dimension d carries a value below d^k, read
               as k digits; Net.split peels the last copy
  FuseB        fusion along a plain wire when one leg bundles k legs
  Unfuse       port-box unfusion

  Assume       transport of a proof across dimensions assumed equal

4. PROOFS OF EQUALITY
---------------------

4.1 Rewrite proofs (archytaszx.semantics.lean_net)

A kernel theorem without free multiplicities is exported over the network semantics. Each
diagram becomes a Net: one node per diagram node, legs labelled by wire, boundary slot i
labelled i (outputs first, then inputs). The theorem states Equal between the start and goal
networks with the diagrams' own scalars. Its proof composes, by Equal.trans and Equal.symm:

  - for a rule step: an isomorphism into a context C ++ L, the rule's lemma applied to L,
    wire_elim for every bare wire the rule leaves, and an isomorphism onto the next network;
  - for an isomorphism step: iso, decided by isoOK.

The scalar identity between the two sides is a linear_combination whose coefficients the
engine computes and Lean's ring checks (archytaszx.semantics.lean_scalar). Scalars outside the
exact model (π, irrational powers, conjugates, sums) become opaque complex variables named in
the theorem's header.

4.2 Families

A family over bang-box multiplicities is a network whose bundled labels carry a chain of
multiplicities. A rule applied inside node boxes is lifted to every multiplicity by
induction, one box at a time, peeling one copy per step; an induction proof of the engine,
nested ones included, becomes a Lean induction over the same peeling. A port box on Z and X
spiders or on a W node's outputs steps by Equal.of_port.

4.3 Proofs by the normaliser (archytaszx.semantics.lean_sym)

Verdicts not reached by rewriting (for example by symbolic contraction) are proved by the
verified normaliser of symbolic sums. Both networks are written as symbolic sums: dimensions as
monomials in dimension symbols, phases as roots of unity times atoms ex g, scalars as constant
expressions. equal_sym proves Equal when the normal form of the scaled difference is empty; the
Lean kernel computes that normal form (decide +kernel), for every value of the dimension symbols
and phase parameters. The normaliser removes one bound variable per move:

  - a variable not used;
  - a variable pinned by a delta whose modulus divides its range;
  - a variable read only through roots of unity of its range, by the character sum
        sum_k omega_D^(k A + B) = D [D | A] omega_D^B;
  - a variable read only through one delta of its range, counted by gcd(a, D).

Canonicalisation substitutes each delta's pivot, merges roots of unity, evaluates constants and
sorts factors. A phase stored at index j requires d > j, which the theorem states as a
hypothesis. sym_check in the exporter mirrors the Lean computation to decide whether to export.

4.4 Assumptions

Dimension equalities a rewrite assumed (deferred constraints) become hypotheses of the
theorem; the proof is carried out over the merged network and transported to the diagrams' own
dimensions (Equal.of_mapDim'). A node whose legs differ in dimension, such as d^p and d^q, is
proved under the hypothesis d^p = d^q. The shell reports every such condition.

4.5 Fallback export

A theorem the network export does not cover (free multiplicities outside the cases above,
pending parameters, dangling ports, disagreeing wire dimensions, or a scalar the exporter cannot
write) is exported without imports: an opaque Diagram type, one constant per diagram, one axiom
per rule instance, isomorphism, instantiation, peeling and context the kernel checked, and the
theorem as their composition. This export records the structure of the proof but does not
re-prove its steps; the shell reports it as "opaque diagrams, every step an axiom".


5. REFUTATIONS
--------------

5.1 Exact refutations (archytaszx.semantics.lean_refute)

A verdict that two diagrams differ is exported as

    ¬ ∀ params, Equal ...

proved at the counterexample (or a default sample point). Both networks are evaluated there by
eval_cyc, computed by the Lean kernel, as a product of (√d)⁻¹ factors times a polynomial in
ζ = om M. At one boundary assignment the scaled difference is rewritten, over the relations of
its atoms and of ζ, into a nonzero integer vector of length at most φ(M); cyc_ne shows that
such a vector does not vanish, by the degree of the cyclotomic minimal polynomial. √p for an
odd prime p is a quadratic Gauss sum, checked by its square and the sign of its real part.

5.2 Numeric refutations (archytaszx.semantics.lean_numeric)

A point involving π, an irrational phase or another value outside ℚ(ζ) is refuted by ball
arithmetic (ArchytasZX.Ball): every subterm receives an enclosure, square roots from their
squares, π from its 20-digit bounds, exponentials from a Taylor bound, and a ball that excludes
zero contradicts the equality.

5.3 Interfaces

Diagrams whose interfaces differ are refuted on their leg dimensions. Families whose boundaries
are written differently are refuted after instantiating their multiplicities.


6. WHAT IS TRUSTED
------------------

    #print axioms main

should list only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. What remains trusted is:

  - Lean and Mathlib;
  - the definitions of the bundled library (the tensors of the generators);
  - the translation of each diagram into a network, which the exported file shows explicitly.

A rule step for which no lemma exists, or an isomorphism the exporter cannot match, becomes an
axiom named unproved_* stating that one step over the same semantics. Such axioms are listed in
the file header and counted by the shell ("lean: tensor-network semantics, N unproved
step(s)"). The constructs that currently have no Lean proof path are listed, with the reason
for each, in LEAN_GAPS.txt.


7. CERTIFIED LANGUAGE RESULTS
-----------------------------

7.1 What is proved

The certify statement and the --certify option (LANGUAGE.txt, section 14.3; SHELL.txt,
section 3.5) write one Lean file per result of a .azx program. Its main theorem has the form

    theorem SCRIPT_NAME (V : LV) (hρ : V.ρ.OK) HYPOTHESES :
        P.sem V = fun ν => axDen V ν R

P is the program as a Prog, R the printed value as an expression. V gives the values of the
dimension symbols (V.ρ.dv), the coefficient atoms and the functions a program calls
(V.fa); ν gives every index variable: output register i is ν i, input register i of a gate
is ν (IN + i), each parameter of the program (a var symbol) is ν (2000 + k). The theorem
holds for every V and every ν, so for every value of the symbols. Its hypotheses state the
range facts used: 2 ≤ d for each dimension, n : ℕ with ρ.dv k = d ^ n for each register of
d^n levels, 1 ≤ n where the result needs it, and one hypothesis per assume statement.

A result of a program containing a loop over a symbolic range holds at every ν for which
the loop counts are nonnegative. An assertion A == B is proved as A.sem V = B.sem V. A
measured distribution is proved as one theorem per branch (the branch's probability is
|<value|psi>|^2), one that the probabilities sum to 1, one that <psi|psi> = 1 and, for a
function of the outcomes, one that every outcome maps to the printed value. The density
matrix a measurement leaves is proved in its vectorised form, [o = o'] psi(o) conj(psi(o')).

7.2 The meaning of a program

The left side is built from the program's syntax trees, never from the engine's results.
Prog has the constructors

  Constructor          Meaning (Prog.sem)
  -------------------  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  leaf e               the value of the expression e: a gate's kernel, a literal state, a
                       scalar or the identity
  app L a b            Σ_y a(o, y) b(y, x) over registers of levels L: U|s>, and A B
  ten k a b            a ⊗ b, a on the first k registers
  mul a b, add a b     product and sum
  inn L a b            Σ_y conj(a(y)) b(y): <a|b>
  cnj a                the complex conjugate
  loop L J lo cnt s i  s applied cnt times to i, iteration t reading J = lo + t

A gate |x>... -> body becomes the kernel of its body: the output kets as deltas on the output
registers, the input registers read as the pattern variables. U^@n on a register of n
qudits, for a one-qudit kernel c ω_d^(α x y), is the digit-wise kernel c^n ω_d^(α ddot(x, y)).
A ket value reduced modulo its register's levels is read as a delta modulo those levels.
Each Lean definition carries the .azx line it translates as a comment.

7.3 Integer atoms

Ket values and phases may contain integer functions: digit products ddot and bitdot,
exclusive or, bits, digit sums, repunits (d^n - 1)/(d - 1), powers of two, the value of a
dimension, the user's functions and powmod. Each is an atom: a variable whose value is the
function of its arguments, each argument reduced modulo its register's levels, so that an
atom is periodic in every variable it reads. User functions and powmod are arbitrary
functions supplied by the valuation (V.fa); no axiom describes them. Exclusive or and bit 0
read modulo 2 are read as a sum and as the variable itself.

7.4 The proof

Prog.sym translates the program into one symbolic sum with atoms; Prog.sym_ok proves it
denotes Prog.sem. The normaliser nfA (LCanon) is proved to keep the value of every term
(nfA_den); the certificate shows that the normal form of the difference between the program
and R is empty. The translation and each term's normal form are stated in the file (SYM,
NF) and checked by the Lean kernel (decide +kernel): the translation against the program,
the normal forms against the difference, and their merge against the empty list.

Besides the moves of section 4.3, the normaliser
  - eliminates a variable an atom reads when a delta pins it and every argument reading it
    is reduced modulo a divisor of the delta's modulus, substituting into the arguments;
  - sums a variable x below N = d^n read only through digit products ddot(x, A_k) and
    linearly in roots of unity of order d (the digit-wise character sum
        Σ_{x<d^n} ω_d^(Σ_i x_i e_i) = Π_{i<n} d [d ∣ e_i]),
    leaving a digit condition, which it reads as a delta when it compares one value with a
    constant or two values;
  - removes digit products and digit conditions with a zero argument, merges atoms that
    compute the same value, reads a reduced argument mod(q, M) as q under a delta or root of
    unity whose modulus divides M, and drops unused atoms;
  - renames bound variables and atoms by first occurrence and sorts the sums.
A loop is proved by induction on its count: the engine's invariant S(J) is shown to equal
the initial state at J = lo and to satisfy S(J + 1) = step_J(S(J)), both by the normaliser
with J free.

7.5 What is trusted

The translation of a program's syntax into Prog (archytaszx.semantics.lean_lang) and of
the printed value into an expression, both shown in the file. The normaliser and the
meaning of Prog are proved in Lean. #print axioms of every certificate theorem lists only
propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound; no certificate uses an axiom of the form
unproved_*. A result outside the certified fragment is reported as "cannot certify:
REASON" and never certified.

7.6 The mirror

archytaszx.semantics.lean_lsym and the mirror functions of lean_lang follow the Lean
definitions on tuples. A certificate is written only when the mirror predicts that the
kernel's check succeeds; --check confirms it with Lean.
