ARCHYTASZX DOCUMENTATION
========================

The Rule Library

Applies to: ArchytasZX 0.19.x
Implementation: archytaszx.rewrite.rules_library (RULES, lookup_rule), with the patterns of
                archytaszx.rewrite.match, match_zxw and match_box


1. CONVENTIONS
--------------

Every rule states an exact equality between its left- and right-hand sides, including the
scalar: the right-hand side is multiplied by the rule's scalar. d is the dimension of the
matched legs; s and t are the dimensions of a connective's split legs. "Phaseless" means the
spider carries no phase vector or the zero one. A state is a spider with no input and one
output; an effect a spider with one input and no output.

Unless stated otherwise, the matched nodes must lie outside every node-scope bang box
(condition outside_every_bang_box), the legs involved must carry exactly equal dimensions
(same_dimension), and the wires consumed must not be claimed by a second wire or a boundary
(joined_and_unclaimed and similar).

Rules are looked up by name with lookup_rule(name); RULES maps every name to its Rule in the
order below. In the shell, ":rules NAME" prints the metadata given here.

Each entry gives:

  Scalar           the factor the rule introduces (per match: computed from the match)
  Quantifiers      the leg-count and dimension variables the equation ranges over
  Guards           arithmetic restrictions on the matched dimension
  Normal-form set  whether the rule belongs to the normal-form set (REWRITE.txt, section 6)
  Side conditions  the conditions every match is checked against, in order


2. RULES
--------

2.1 spider_fusion
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Two spiders of the same colour joined by a wire are one spider: its legs are the surviving legs
of the lower-id spider followed by those of the other, and its phase vector is the sum of
theirs. Z spiders may be joined in any direction; X spiders by an output-to-input wire. Any
further wire joining the pair survives as a self-loop on the fused spider.

  Scalar:           1
  Quantifiers:      leg counts m_a, n_a, m_b, n_b; dimensions d
  Guards:           none
  Normal-form set:  yes
  Side conditions:  distinct_nodes, same_generator_type, parallel_wires_become_self_loops,
                    consumed_wire_direction_permitted_for_color, consumed_ports_singly_claimed,
                    bang_box_scope_agreement, dimension_agreement, phase_dimension_agreement,
                    dimension_guards_satisfied


2.2 z_fusion_prime_d
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Spider fusion restricted to a prime shared dimension: same builder and scalar as spider_fusion.
A composite or symbolic shared dimension is not a match.

  Scalar:           1
  Quantifiers:      leg counts m_a, n_a, m_b, n_b; dimensions d
  Guards:           prime
  Normal-form set:  no
  Side conditions:  distinct_nodes, same_generator_type, parallel_wires_become_self_loops,
                    consumed_wire_direction_permitted_for_color, consumed_ports_singly_claimed,
                    bang_box_scope_agreement, dimension_agreement, phase_dimension_agreement,
                    dimension_guards_satisfied


2.3 fourier_cancellation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Four Fourier boxes in series are the identity wire: F^4 = id.

  Scalar:           1
  Quantifiers:      leg counts none; dimensions d
  Guards:           none
  Normal-form set:  yes
  Side conditions:  chain_is_series, same_dimension


2.4 zx_cap
~~~~~~~~~~

A phaseless Z state wired into a phaseless X effect is the empty diagram times d^(1/2). The X
effect contracts by the character sum to sqrt(d) [k == 0 mod d], and summing that against the Z
state's all-ones vector leaves sqrt(d).

  Scalar:           sqrt(d)
  Quantifiers:      leg counts none; dimensions d
  Guards:           none
  Normal-form set:  yes
  Side conditions:  state_is_a_z_spider, effect_is_an_x_spider, joined_and_unclaimed, phaseless,
                    same_dimension, outside_every_bang_box


2.5 identity_removal
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

A phaseless spider with one input and one output is the identity on its wire, and is removed.

  Scalar:           1
  Quantifiers:      leg counts none; dimensions d
  Guards:           none
  Normal-form set:  yes
  Side conditions:  node_is_a_phaseless_spider, one_in_one_out, not_a_self_loop,
                    legs_singly_claimed, splice_keeps_two_ports, same_dimension,
                    leaves_every_bang_box_populated, no_port_boxed_leg, splice_keeps_copies


2.6 triangle_inverse_cancellation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

A triangle T and its inverse Ti in series, in either order, are the identity wire at every
dimension.

  Scalar:           1
  Quantifiers:      leg counts none; dimensions d
  Guards:           none
  Normal-form set:  yes
  Side conditions:  inverse_pair_in_series, joining_wire_unclaimed, outer_legs_singly_claimed,
                    splice_keeps_two_ports, same_dimension, bang_box_scope_agreement,
                    leaves_every_bang_box_populated


2.7 state_copy
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

A phaseless X state wired into a phaseless Z spider with n outputs is n phaseless X states.
Since X_{0->1} = sqrt(d)|0>, the factor is d^((1 - n)/2), computed per match from n.

  Scalar:           d^((1 - n)/2), per match (sqrt(d) at n = 0)
  Quantifiers:      leg counts n; dimensions d
  Guards:           none
  Normal-form set:  yes
  Side conditions:  state_is_a_phaseless_x_spider, spider_is_a_phaseless_z_spider,
                    joined_and_unclaimed, same_dimension, outside_every_bang_box


2.8 hopf
~~~~~~~~

A Z spider and an X spider joined by one plain wire and by one wire through F^2 (the antipode)
fall apart into the two spiders with their remaining legs, times d^(-1). A plain double edge
without the F pair is not a match.

  Scalar:           1/d
  Quantifiers:      leg counts m, n, p, q; dimensions d
  Guards:           none
  Normal-form set:  yes
  Side conditions:  z_and_x_spiders, two_paths, path_ports_unclaimed, same_dimension,
                    phase_dimension_agreement, outside_every_bang_box


2.9 bialgebra
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

X_{2->1} wired into Z_{1->2}, both phaseless, crosses over into two Z_{1->2} and two X_{2->1},
times d^(1/2). This rule grows the diagram.

  Scalar:           sqrt(d)
  Quantifiers:      leg counts none; dimensions d
  Guards:           none
  Normal-form set:  no
  Side conditions:  x_is_a_phaseless_two_to_one, z_is_a_phaseless_one_to_two,
                    joined_and_unclaimed, same_dimension, outside_every_bang_box


2.10 fourier_state_color_change
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

A Fourier box on the leg of a phaseless Z state is a phaseless X state: F (sum_k |k>) =
sqrt(d)|0> = X_{0->1}. The dual form, a Fourier box on a phaseless Z effect, is covered too.

  Scalar:           1
  Quantifiers:      leg counts none; dimensions d
  Guards:           none
  Normal-form set:  yes
  Side conditions:  spider_is_a_phaseless_z_state, fourier_box_on_that_leg,
                    joined_and_unclaimed, free_leg_singly_claimed, same_dimension,
                    bang_box_scope_agreement

2.16 w_fusion
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

  Normal-form set:  yes
  Side conditions:  two_w_nodes, joining_wire_unclaimed, same_dimension,

2.18 w_zero_copy
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

A phaseless X state wired into a W node with n outputs is n phaseless X states, times d^((1 -
n)/2), computed per match: W|0> = |0...0> and X_{0->1} = sqrt(d)|0>.

  Scalar:           d^((1 - n)/2), per match (sqrt(d) at n = 0)
  Quantifiers:      leg counts n; dimensions d
  Guards:           none
  Normal-form set:  yes
  Side conditions:  state_is_a_phaseless_x_spider, target_is_a_w_node, joined_and_unclaimed,
                    same_dimension, outside_every_bang_box

3. SOUNDNESS
------------

Each rule's equation, scalar included, is checked against the numeric oracle by the test
suite over sweeps of dimensions and leg counts, and most rules are lemmas of the bundled Lean
library (LEAN.txt); the rules and constructs without a Lean lemma are listed in LEAN_GAPS.txt.
A rewrite that changes the denoted linear map, scalar included, is a soundness bug; see
CONTRIBUTING.md for how to report one.
