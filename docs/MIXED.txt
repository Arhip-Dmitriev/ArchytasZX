ARCHYTASZX DOCUMENTATION
========================

Mixed States: Discard and the CPM Construction

Applies to: ArchytasZX 2.0.x
Implementation: archytaszx.diagram.mixed (conj, double, purify, constructions),
                archytaszx.rewrite.rules_discard (discard rules), archytaszx.semantics
                (denote_mixed, contract_mixed_symbolic, compare, decide_equal, prove),
                archytaszx/lean/ArchytasZX/Mixed.lean (Lean semantics)
Sources:        [CJPV] T. Carette, E. Jeandel, S. Perdrix, R. Vilmart, "Completeness of
                graphical languages for mixed states quantum mechanics", ICALP 2019,
                arXiv:1902.07143; axioms read from the source in
                archytaszx/papers/carette2019mixed/AXIOMS.txt.
                [CK] B. Coecke, A. Kissinger, "Picturing Quantum Processes", CUP 2017, ch. 6.
                [P1], [P2] as in COMPLETENESS.txt.
                [BB] J.-L. Brylinski, R. Brylinski, "Universal quantum gates",
                arXiv:quant-ph/0108062.


1. PURPOSE
----------

A pure diagram denotes one linear map. A mixed diagram may also contain discard nodes G and
denotes a completely positive map (CPM) between finite-dimensional systems, for qudits at every
d and for qufinite (mixed-dimension) diagrams. Mixed states, channels, partial traces and
measurements with forgotten outcomes are written with G.


2. SEMANTICS
------------

2.1 Conjugation. conj(D) is D with every node replaced by its entrywise complex conjugate,
wiring, boundary and leg order unchanged, scalar conjugated:

  Generator                      In conj(D)
  -----------------------------  -----------------------------------------------
  Z, phase vector alpha          Z, phase vector -alpha
  Z, weights a                   Z, weights conj(a)
  X, phase vector alpha          X, phase vector k -> -alpha_{-k mod d}
  X, weights a                   X, weights k -> conj(a_{-k mod d})
  F                              F^3 (= F^dagger), three F nodes in series
  T, Ti, W, B, S, G              unchanged (real tensors)

At a symbolic dimension the reflected index of an X spider's entries is written with the
exact atom ModDelta (ALGEBRA.txt). Every row was checked with the oracle at d = 2..7 (B and S
at (s, t) = (2, 3), (3, 2), (2, 2), (3, 4)), with phase entries, weights and rules, at
concrete and symbolic dimensions: 350 instances, no mismatch.

2.2 The discard G. One leg of any dimension, no phase. As an input it is the trace effect;
as an output the unnormalised maximally mixed state. G has no pure denotation: denote refuses
it and names double().

2.3 Doubling (the definition). double(D) is the pure diagram made of:

  1. a copy D0 of D without its G nodes and a copy D1 of conj(D) without its G nodes;
  2. for every G attached by its leg to a port p, one wire between p in D0 and p in D1 (the
     trace); a G in a bang box instead becomes a phase-free two-leg Z in that box;
  3. a G on the boundary becomes a phase-free two-leg Z (the cup or cap); a G wired to a G a
     phase-free zero-leg Z over the leg dimension (the scalar d);
  4. boundary: D0 outputs, D1 outputs, D0 inputs, D1 inputs;
  5. scalar c * conj(c);
  6. each node-scope bang box as one box over both copies, each port-scope box as one box per
     copy, all with the original multiplicity symbol.

With T = denote(double(D)) of axes (o, o', i, i'), D is the CPM
Phi_D(rho)[o, o'] = sum_{i, i'} T[o, o', i, i'] rho[i, i']. A pure U gives rho -> U rho U^dagger;
G gives the trace. Every node of double(D) maps back to D (double_with_origin).

2.4 Equality. Mixed diagrams with the same boundary are equal iff their doubled diagrams are
equal. On CPMs UP_TO_GLOBAL_PHASE coincides with EXACT; compare runs it as EXACT and says
so. UP_TO_POSITIVE_SCALAR (b = lambda a, lambda > 0) is used only when requested.

2.5 The mixed scalar. A mixed diagram keeps a pure scalar c; its CPM scalar is |c|^2. The
comparison view of a mixed diagram (normal forms, search states, the kernel's iso) carries
c * conj(c). A rule's pure scalar s multiplies the CPM by |s|^2.

2.6 Facts, checked with the oracle at d = 2..5 on random diagrams with G nodes (scripts kept
out of the tree; counts are instances, none failed):

  double(D) against the Stinespring form of purify(D)                     176
  (F1) double(conj D) = double(D) with the two copies swapped               160
  (F2) double(E . D) = double(E) . double(D); tensor up to the permutation   68, 135
  (F3) pure D: double(D) = D (x) conj(D) up to the boundary permutation      80
  (F4) pure L = R exactly: double(C[L]) = double(C[R])                       24
  (F5) pure D, lambda D: equal iff |lambda| = 1                             160
  (F6) G after an isometry (Z phase, F, Z (1 -> 2, 3), X (1 -> 2), S)        32
  channel(purify(D)) = D                                                    160
  bang boxes: double commutes with expansion at n = 0..3                    20

Lean proves (F1), (F3), (F4), (F5) and (F6) for the Stinespring CPM of Mixed.lean
(LEAN.txt).


3. G AND THE CONSTRUCTIONS
--------------------------

archytaszx.diagram.mixed:

    is_pure(D), discards(D)            no G node; the G nodes
    conj(D), conj_with_origin(D)       section 2.1
    double(D), double_with_origin(D)   section 2.3
    purify(D) -> (P, env)              Stinespring form: every G removed, its leg moved to
                                       the end of the boundary outputs (env positions); a G on
                                       an input port or a boundary output becomes a cup, a G on
                                       a boundary input an identity Z, a G-G pair a cup
    channel(P, env)                    the inverse: P with the env outputs discarded
    partial_trace(D, legs)             G on the boundary outputs at positions legs
    compose(D, E), tensor(*Ds)         sequential and parallel composition

    discard(d)                         G effect
    maximally_mixed(d)                 G state, scalar 1/sqrt(d): the CPM id/d
    dephase(d), measure_z(d)           Z (1 -> 2), second output discarded
    measure_x(d)                       X (1 -> 2), second output discarded = F . dephase . F^dagger
    outcome(k, d)                      the pure effect <k| = d^(-1/2) X (1 -> 0) phase jk/d;
                                       closed with a state through double, the Born probability

mixture(states, probs) is not built: [P1] constructs no sum of arbitrary diagrams (its
conclusion attributes controlled summation to later work); its only sum is the normal form's
sum over basis kets, which needs the states' amplitudes, and the engine has no W sum of
diagrams.

archytaszx.semantics: denote.denote_mixed(D) (the CPM tensor T[o, o', i, i'], numeric, an
oracle only), contract_symbolic.contract_mixed_symbolic(D) (the doubled symbolic contraction,
every dimension and multiplicity formal). Applying a channel to a state is composition.


4. RULES
--------

4.1 Lifted pure rules. Every rule of rules_library.RULES applies to a mixed diagram at a match
holding no G: no pattern matches G (template matching places G only where a template names
one; boundary insertion never copies a G node), and the kernel refuses a pure rule's step
that consumes a G. By (F4) such a step is sound. On random mixed diagrams every RULES entry
was applied at every match: 4369 applications, no G consumed, no doubled-oracle mismatch.

4.2 Discard rules (archytaszx.rewrite.rules_discard.MIXED_RULES, RULES.txt section 9): the
equation G . V = G of [CJPV] for the qudit analogue of each element of its ZX spanning set
(e^{i alpha}, |0>, H, R_Z(alpha), CNot), under COMPLETENESS.txt section 2, and for the [P2]
connectives:

  discard_zero        G . |0> = empty, |0> the pink dot state; scalar sqrt(d)
  discard_fourier     G . F = G
  discard_phase       G . Z (1 -> 1, phases only) = G
  discard_cx          (G (x) G) . CX = G (x) G, CX = Z (1 -> 2) into pink (2 -> 1); scalar
                      d^(-1/2) for the engine's Z, X pair
  discard_binder      G . B (s, t) = G (x) G (concrete s, t)
  discard_splitter    (G (x) G) . S (s, t) = G (concrete s, t)
  discard_spider      derived: G^(x n) . Z (1 -> n, phases only) = G, n >= 1
  discard_merge       derived: two discarded legs of one Z spider are one
  discard_loop        derived: G joined to G = empty; scalar sqrt(d) (CPM d)

with the converses discard_zero_converse, discard_fourier_converse, discard_phase_converse
(free phase), discard_cx_converse, discard_binder_converse, discard_splitter_converse. The
[CJPV] phase axiom e^{i alpha} = empty is the scalar convention of 2.5. G matches either
orientation. Every rule was checked with the oracle on doubled diagrams at d = 2..12, arities
up to 4, both G orientations, in random pure contexts: 990 instances, no mismatch.


5. DECIDING AND PROVING
-----------------------

5.1 DOUBLED. decide_equal on mixed operands (a G on either side, or mixed=True) runs the
pure ladder unchanged on both doubled diagrams and reports DecisionMethod.DOUBLED with the
settling rung in inner_method and the doubled pair in doubled. compare doubles mixed
operands. prove_exhaustive, and prove when NATIVE finds nothing, prove the doubled pair and
return a MixedCertificate of kind DOUBLED (both mixed diagrams, both doubled diagrams with the
origin of every node, the inner pure proof); check_mixed re-doubles both sides, requires them
identical id for id to the stored ones, and checks the inner proof.

5.2 NATIVE. prove first searches with tactics.mixed_moves (the fixpoint of
mixed_normal_form_rules, then every discard rule and every saturation rule) and returns a
MixedCertificate of kind NATIVE: single-sheet steps, lifted pure steps and discard steps,
replayed and re-checked by the kernel. normal_form on a mixed diagram runs
mixed_normal_form_rules: the forward discard rules first, then the pure normal-form rules;
sound, not complete.

5.3 Completeness of the DOUBLED route. Let D1, D2 be mixed diagrams at concrete dimensions
without bang boxes, with equal CPMs. By definition 2.3 and 2.4, double(D1) and double(D2) are
pure diagrams at concrete dimensions, without bang boxes, built from the engine's generators,
denoting the same linear map. By COMPLETENESS.txt section 1 there is a derivation between
them by the rules of RULES; exhaustive mode searches for it. Hence every true equation of
mixed diagrams in this regime has a DOUBLED certificate.


6. THE [CJPV] THEOREM AND NATIVE COMPLETENESS
---------------------------------------------

6.1 The theorem, as printed ([CJPV] Corollary cor:ground, from Theorem thm:ground): "Given a
dagger-CC C with enough isometries, if G is a dagger-CC universal complete graphical language
for C then G^ground is a universal complete language for CPM(C)." G^ground is G with one new
generator ground and one equation ground . D = ground per isometry D of G; given a spanning
set of the isometries, one equation per element suffices (the recipe after cor:ground). The
statement is not restricted to qubits; the paper's examples are qubit languages (ZX, its
pi/2 fragment, ZW, ZH), and for Clifford+T the hypothesis fails (Proposition lem:CPC).

6.2 The hypotheses, for the engine at a concrete dimension d (C = Qudit_d, the full
subcategory of FHilb on the C^(d^n)) and for qufinite diagrams (C = the full subcategory on
tensor products of C^(d_i)).

  (H1) A dagger-compact universal complete language. Universality and completeness: [P1] for
       every d >= 2, [P2] for qufinite, transferred to the engine's rules at concrete
       dimensions without bang boxes (COMPLETENESS.txt, sections 1 and 4). Compact: wires are
       direction-free. Dagger: each generator's adjoint is a diagram (Z and X with negated
       phases and conjugated weights, legs reversed; F^dagger = F^3; W, T, Ti, B, S
       transposed through bent wires), well defined on equivalence classes by completeness.
  (H2) Enough isometries. [CJPV]'s proof for Qubit (Proposition lem:CPQ) applies verbatim:
       by Stinespring (Proposition lem:CPFH) two purifications of one CPM are related by
       isometries u, v into some C^m; post-composing with an isometry C^m -> C^(d^n), d^n >= m,
       puts their codomain in Qudit_d. For qufinite, C^m is itself an object. Hence both
       categories have enough isometries, and by lem:thm:cpenv their discard constructions
       are CPM(C).
  (H3) The interpretation is faithful (completeness) and full (universality); a faithful
       dagger functor reflects isometries (F(f)^dagger F(f) = 1 = F(1) gives f^dagger f = 1),
       so its restriction to isometries is surjective, as thm:ground requires.

6.3 A finite spanning set. Qudit_d, concrete d >= 2. Claim: every isometry V : C^(d^n) ->
C^(d^m) is a composite of tensor products of identities, swaps, and the elements of
S_d = (e^{i alpha}, |0>, F, F^3, Z (1 -> 1) with phases, CX).

  (a) V = U . (1 (x) |0>^(x (m - n))) for a unitary U on C^(d^m) (complete V's orthonormal
      columns to a basis).
  (b) One qudit: U(d) is generated by the diagonal unitaries T (Z phase gates times
      e^{i alpha}) and F T F^3. Let H be the subgroup they generate; H is path-connected, so
      by Yamabe's theorem an analytic subgroup of U(d) whose Lie algebra h contains the
      diagonal t and F t F^{-1}, the anti-Hermitian circulants. The complexification of h
      contains every diagonal matrix and the cyclic shift S = sum_i E_{i+1, i}. For a diagonal
      h with entries a_i = 2^i the components of S under ad h have the distinct eigenvalues
      a_{i+1} - a_i (indices mod d), so each E_{i+1, i} lies in h_C, and likewise each
      E_{i, i+1} (from S^{-1}). These generate gl_d, so h = u(d) and H = U(d); every element
      of H is a finite product.
  (c) n >= 2 qudits: [BB] (abstract and main theorem): the one-qudit gates together with a
      two-qudit gate V produce all n-qudit gates exactly iff V is not primitive (does not map
      every decomposable tensor to a decomposable tensor). CX is not primitive: it maps
      F|0> (x) |0> to sum_k |k, k> / sqrt(d).

  From G . g = G for every g in S_d, G . V = G follows for every isometry V by induction on
  its decomposition: G^(x m) . (g (x) 1) = (G . g) (x) G^(x .) = G^(x .). In the engine:
  discard_zero, discard_fourier (three times for F^3), discard_phase, discard_cx and the
  scalar convention, with their converses for reading the equations right to left.

  Qufinite. B and S are unitary, so with them every unitary on C^(d_1) (x) ... (x) C^(d_k) is
  S . U . B for a unitary U on C^(prod d_i), generated by (b) at that dimension. An isometry
  C^a -> C^b with a dividing b is B . (1 (x) |0>_(b/a)) up to such unitaries. An isometry
  C^a -> C^b with a not dividing b is not: every composite of the elements above and
  ancillas |0>_e maps C^a into a space of dimension a * prod e. The canonical injection
  iota_{a,b} (the phaseless [P2] mixed-dimensional Z spider with legs a and b) must be added,
  with the equation G_b . iota_{a,b} = G_a.

6.4 The argument of thm:ground, with these sets. If CPM(D1) = CPM(D2), let Pi be the
purifications (purify). By (H2), P1 and P2 are related by the transitive closure of
(1 (x) u) P1 = (1 (x) v) P2 with isometries u, v. Each link is: D1 = (1 (x) G) P1, read
right to left through the converse discard rules to (1 (x) G)(1 (x) u) P1 (u decomposed by
6.3), then (1 (x) u) P1 = (1 (x) v) P2 by pure rules (completeness, COMPLETENESS.txt), then
forward discard rules to (1 (x) G) P2 = D2.

6.5 Outcome (ODOACER D.4 (ii)).

  Qudit, concrete d, no bang boxes: native completeness holds. If two mixed diagrams denote
  the same CPM, there is a derivation between them by the rules of RULES and MIXED_RULES
  (discard_zero, discard_fourier, discard_phase, discard_cx and their converses suffice
  among the latter), mixed scalars compared by modulus. Proof: 6.2 to 6.4, resting on
  [CJPV] thm:ground, [P1] (through COMPLETENESS.txt), Stinespring, Yamabe's theorem and
  [BB].

  Qufinite, no bang boxes: native completeness holds for the rule set extended by
  G_b . iota_{a,b} = G_a for every a < b with a not dividing b; the engine registers no such
  rule, so qufinite native completeness is not claimed for MIXED_RULES. The DOUBLED route
  (5.3) is complete for qufinite diagrams.

  Symbolic dimensions or bang boxes: as for pure diagrams, no completeness is claimed. The
  NATIVE search is sound and incomplete; DOUBLED decisions are sound.

  In every regime the engine's guarantee for mixed equality includes 5.3.


7. SOURCES AND LITERATURE
-------------------------

[CJPV] was fetched from arXiv (1902.07143, v1, the only version) into
references/Papers/carette2019mixed/ (not tracked); its definitions, theorems and the ZX, ZW
and ZH discard axioms are in archytaszx/papers/carette2019mixed/AXIOMS.txt. [BB] was read at
its arXiv abstract page.

A search on 2026-11-06 for later work on qudit or qufinite ZXW with discard ("ZXW discard",
"qudit mixed state completeness ZX", "qufinite ZXW mixed states discard") found none. Related
results found: the discard construction applied to the qudit stabiliser ZX-calculus in odd
prime dimensions (arXiv:2204.12531); a complete language for Hermiticity-preserving
superoperators by an extension of the ZW-calculus (arXiv:2302.04212); the decohered
ZX-calculus (arXiv:2508.04296), which treats the discard ZX-calculus for qubits; the
completeness of the finite-dimensional ZX-calculus (arXiv:2405.10896), pure.


8. LIMITS
---------

  - Symbolic multiplicities: a bang box of discards meeting the rest of the diagram at wires
    into Z or X spiders contracts symbolically on the doubled pair (SEMANTICS.txt, section 5):
    induct proves examples/mixed/born_ghz.azx and partial_trace_ghz.azx over n, the step by
    symbolic contraction, with no re-checkable certificate. A box meeting other nodes at a
    wire stays outside symbolic contraction. Removing such a box needs a sheet-level rule.
  - The qufinite injection rule of 6.5 is not registered.
  - Lean: the discard rules are proved as tensor identities (Mixed.lean); a NATIVE
    certificate exports through the Lean-proved normaliser of symbolic sums on the doubled
    pair when it applies, otherwise opaquely with each discard step an axiom
    unproved_DISCARD_<rule>_N (LEAN.txt).
