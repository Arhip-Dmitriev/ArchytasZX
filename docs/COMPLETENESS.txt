ARCHYTASZX DOCUMENTATION
========================

Completeness of the Rule System

Applies to: ArchytasZX 2.0.x
Implementation: archytaszx.rewrite.rules_axioms (the axioms as rules), archytaszx.rewrite
                match_template (template matching), archytaszx.rewrite.exhaustive and
                archytaszx.semantics.prove.prove_exhaustive (exhaustive mode)
Sources:        [P1] B. Poor, Q. Wang, R. A. Shaikh, L. Yeh, R. Yeung, B. Coecke,
                "Completeness for arbitrary finite dimensions of ZXW-calculus, a unifying
                calculus", LICS 2023, arXiv:2302.12135 (REFERENCES.bib: poor2023zxw).
                [P2] Q. Wang, B. Poor, R. A. Shaikh, "Completeness of qufinite ZXW calculus,
                a graphical language for finite-dimensional quantum theory", arXiv:2309.13014
                (REFERENCES.bib: wang2023qufinitezxw).
                Both papers, their sources and the axiom lists read from them are in
                archytaszx/papers/poor2023zxw/ and archytaszx/papers/wang2023qufinitezxw/ (AXIOMS.txt).


1. THE CLAIM
------------

Let D1 and D2 be two diagrams with the same boundary, without bang boxes, all of whose
dimensions are concrete (literal, or symbols bound in the parameter environment), built from
the generators Z, X (with phases and complex weights), F, T, Ti, W, B and S. If D1 and D2
denote the same linear map, scalar included, then there is a derivation from D1 to D2 by
applications of the rules registered in archytaszx.rewrite.rules_library.RULES. Each step is
an application of a rule at a match its pattern verifies, with the rule's free values set
where it has any (RULES.txt, section 1), so the engine replays the derivation as a
certificate and the proof kernel re-checks it.

In particular this holds for every pair of [P1] qudit ZXW diagrams at a fixed dimension d and
every pair of [P2] qufinite ZXW diagrams, through the translation of section 2.

The claim is an existence statement proved in section 4 from the completeness theorems of
[P1] (Theorem: every qudit ZXW diagram rewrites to a unique normal form) and [P2] (the same
for qufinite diagrams). It is not a decision procedure. The automatic search (normal form,
saturation, tactics) remains sound and incomplete (PROOFS.txt); the exhaustive mode of
section 6 searches for such derivations without bound.

Lean. Seventeen rules added for this claim are proved in Lean by existing rule lemmas in the
instances listed in section 3 (LEAN.txt, section 4.1); every other step of them exports as a
named axiom unproved_RULE_N (LEAN_GAPS.txt, item 9). The generator mapping of section 2 is
proved in Lean for every dimension (ArchytasZX.Paper) except for the Z box with complex
weights, which the Lean semantics does not have. The derivations of section 4 are therefore
kernel-checked and, except for those steps, Lean-checked.


2. THE GENERATOR MAPPING
------------------------

Conventions of both papers: diagrams are read top to bottom; omega = e^(2 pi i/d); indices
are taken mod d. The engine's conventions are in SEMANTICS.txt, section 2: F[j][k] =
omega^(jk)/sqrt(d), the X spider is F^(x)m . Z . (F^dagger)^(x)n, wires are direction-free
contractions (a cap or cup is a wire, the swap is wiring).

Every entry was checked numerically at d = 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 (splitter and merger at (s, t) =
(2, 3), (3, 2), (2, 2), (3, 4)) against the matrix the paper gives, by contracting the
engine's diagram.

3. THE AXIOMS
-------------

rules that differ from [P1]'s), together with a converse rule reading it right to left.
Every rule was checked against the numeric oracle on random instances of its matched side,
alone and inside random weighted contexts, at d = 2..6 (rules defined at a concrete dimension
at d = 2..4, mixed-dimensional rules at the listed dimension tuples); every rule's step was
replayed as a certificate and re-checked by the proof kernel. No mismatch was found.

Status columns: O = oracle-checked, K = replayed and kernel-checked, Lean: L = proved by the
named lemma, U = exported as the unproved axiom unproved_RULE_N; "L/U" gives the rule then its
converse.

4. TRANSFER OF THE COMPLETENESS THEOREMS
----------------------------------------

4.1 Translation. Let tau map a paper diagram to an engine diagram by section 2: each
generator to its engine counterpart, wiring to wiring, a bare boundary-to-boundary wire to a
phaseless Z spider with one input and one output, and a closed scalar diagram to the scalar
it denotes. tau preserves the denoted map exactly (section 2). Conversely every engine
diagram D in the scope of section 1 is reached from the paper diagram pi(D) obtained by
replacing each X spider, T, Ti, phase vector, B and scalar factor by its definition: the
definitional rules of section 3.3 and binder_definition rewrite D to tau(pi(D)).

4.2 Axioms. For every axiom L = R of [P1] or [P2] and every context C, the engine derives
tau(C[R]) from tau(C[L]) and tau(C[L]) from tau(C[R]):

  - the rule of the axiom, or its converse, applies at the image of the axiom's instance:
    template matching is up to the leg symmetry the papers assume (Z boxes flexsymmetric,
    (2.9); W outputs symmetric, (2.10); X spiders symmetric within inputs and within outputs);
  - a notation the axiom uses in a form the template does not (H^dagger, D, pink spiders,
    scalar diagrams) is first brought into the template's form by the definitional rules and
    zxw_p1, both of which have converses;
  - a converse with free values (S1, AD, Ept, ZV, scalar_box) is applied with the values the
    paper's instance has ("apply ... with NAME = VALUE"); the pattern verifies the match with
    those values, so the step replays;
  - an identity inserted by a paper step on any wire, internal or at the boundary, is
    zxw_s2_converse or zxw_s2_pink_converse;
  - leg order within a direction is identified by the kernel's iso inference; bending a Z leg
    between directions is qf_z_flex; permuting W outputs is qf_w_sym and zxw_sym.

4.3 Theorem. If D1 and D2 (section 1) denote the same map, then pi(D1) and pi(D2) denote the
same map, so by [P1] (one dimension) or [P2] (several) there is a paper derivation
pi(D1) = E_0 = E_1 = ... = E_k = pi(D2), each step one axiom instance in a context, read in
either direction. By 4.2 each step lifts to an engine derivation tau(E_i) ->* tau(E_{i+1});
by 4.1, D1 ->* tau(pi(D1)) and tau(pi(D2)) <-* D2, the latter read backwards through the
converses of the definitional rules. Concatenating gives a derivation D1 ->* D2 by
registered rules, every step replayable.

4.4 Lean. ArchytasZX.Paper proves the mapping of section 2 for every d: paperH_eq (H = F),
paperZ_eq (green circle spider), paperW_eq (W node), paperT_eq and paperTi_eq (triangles),
paperSplit_eq (splitter), paperD_eq (D = F F), and pink_eq (the pink K_j spider is
d^((m+n)/2 - 1) times the X spider with phases j k/d). Not proved: the Z box with complex
weights (outside the Lean semantics), and the statement of each axiom in paper conventions:
the rule lemmas are local equations between labelled networks (LocalEq), and restating them
over the paper* tensors needs an evaluation of each axiom's network, which is not written.

4.5 What is proved and what is checked. The transfer is the argument above; it relies on
the completeness theorems of [P1] and [P2] as published. What the engine checks is each rule
(oracle, d = 2..6) and each step (replay and kernel). No complete derivation between two
arbitrary diagrams is constructed by this argument; finding one is the job of section 6.


5. CLAIM (A): THE PAPERS' LEMMAS
--------------------------------

Target: the automatic search (decide and prove, naming no rule) finds a derivation of every
lemma [P1] and [P2] derive from their axioms.

The lemmas are listed in archytaszx/papers/poor2023zxw/LEMMAS.txt ([P1] Lemmas 7 to 47) and
archytaszx/papers/wang2023qufinitezxw/LEMMAS.txt ([P2] Lemmas A.1 to A.80), each with the steps its
proof prints. Twenty [P1] lemmas are encoded at the literal dimension d = 3 under
examples/completeness/lemmas/ (one file each: the two sides as @lhs and @rhs, prove, then
certificate kernel); section 9 gives the corpus run.

Status: claim (A) is not established. The automatic search proves 13 of the 20 encoded
lemmas; the remaining 7 and the lemmas not encoded are open (section 9).


6. CLAIM (B): EXHAUSTIVE MODE
-----------------------------

    prove exhaustive [against] [TARGET]

runs the interface check and the oracle refutation rung of prove (PROOFS.txt), then a
breadth-first search from both diagrams at once over every registered rule, automatic or
not, converses included (archytaszx.rewrite.exhaustive). Every match each pattern enumerates
is applied to every diagram of the current layer; a diagram whose comparison view is
isomorphic to one already reached on its side is pruned. The start side's layer k is expanded
before the goal side's layer k, and both before layer k + 1 of either. When a diagram reached
from one side is isomorphic to one reached from the other, the two paths form an ordinary
proof certificate, checked as prove's are. There is no depth cap and no time limit.

Fairness. A derivation of length n each of whose steps is the application of a rule at a
match its pattern enumerates (free values at their defaults) is found by the time the start
side has expanded layer n. When every step of its last floor(n/2) steps uses a rule with a
converse, it is found by the time the start side has expanded layer ceil(n/2) and the goal
side layer floor(n/2): that half, read from the goal, is a sequence of converse applications.
Every rule has a converse except connective_inverse_bind_first (2.22) and port_box_unfusion
(2.28, bang boxes only).

Termination.
  - Unequal diagrams at concrete dimensions: the oracle rung contracts both and reports
    UNEQUAL before the search starts.
  - Equal diagrams joined by a derivation of enumerated steps: the search terminates with a
    proof.
  - Equal diagrams every derivation of which needs a free value away from its default (a
    nontrivial split in S1 or AD read right to left, an inserted Ept or scalar box of chosen
    parameter): the search does not terminate. Section 4 guarantees a derivation exists, not
    that one exists with default free values; such derivations are applied by hand with
    "apply ... with".
  - Diagrams with bang boxes or symbolic dimensions: outside the claim; the search may not
    terminate.
If both sides close (every diagram reachable already reached) without meeting, the result
is NOT_FOUND.


7. LIMITS
---------

  - Concrete dimensions only. Rules stated at symbolic d where the paper's shape does not
    depend on d; the V, M, HD, VA, ZV, VW, KZ, D1 and K2 families and every mixed-dimensional
    rule need a concrete dimension (literal, or bound in the parameter environment).
  - No bang boxes: template rules never match a node or leg inside a bang box.
  - Derivability, not decision; the automatic layers use only the rules section 8 lists.
  - The mixed-dimensional S1 of [P2] has no converse rule; its unfusion is not enumerated.
  - connective_inverse_bind_first and port_box_unfusion have no converse rules; exhaustive mode
    reads them only forwards from either side (section 6).
  - A paper diagram that is a bare wire is represented by a phaseless Z spider with two
    legs; a non-Z port facing a boundary entry of the other direction carries such a spider
    as an adapter. Equalities hold modulo these representatives.
  - Lean: section 1.


8. AUTOMATION
-------------

Normal form. zxw_ept and scalar_box joined the normal-form set, after every other rule. Each
removes a node and never adds one, and zxw_ept removes a wire: with them every rule of the set
decreases the pair (wire count, node count) lexicographically, so normal forms still
terminate (archytaszx.rewrite.engine.NORMAL_FORM_RULE_NAMES).

Saturation. The e-graph has a backoff rule scheduler (as in egg): a rule applied more than
match_limit << b times in one iteration, b its bans so far, is cut for the rest of the
iteration and banned for ban_length << b iterations (SaturationLimits.match_limit = 1000,
ban_length = 5); an e-node expanded while a rule is banned is expanded under it once the ban
ends. With no ban, the order of applications is as before.

Promotion into saturation and tactics (automatic = True): zxw_ept, scalar_box, zxw_k0,
zxw_k2, zxw_zer, zxw_ad, zxw_bs0, zxw_bsj, zxw_ta, zxw_bzw, zxw_aso, zxw_hd, zxw_va,
zxw_zv, zxw_kz, qf_dzx, qf_e, qf_k0, qf_bzw, qf_s1: the axioms read in the direction that
does not grow a diagram. Acceptance: the regression set of the section-6 baseline (the example
command files, a command battery exercising every older rule, and the Lean checks of the
files they write) gives output identical to the run before promotion, and the automatic
corpus result went from 9 to 13 of 20.

Kept out of automation: every converse and every rule that grows a diagram or permutes legs
(zxw_s1 and its converse, zxw_s2 family converses, zxw_k1, zxw_pcy, zxw_sym, zxw_ww, zxw_vw,
zxw_d1, zxw_h1, zxw_p1, the qf_ rules not listed above, the definitional rules): an insertion
or unfusion applies at every wire or leg mask and would exhaust the saturation budget on
every input; a permutation or colour change undoes another at the next step. They stay
available to apply, matches, certificates, the kernel, the Lean export and exhaustive mode.
