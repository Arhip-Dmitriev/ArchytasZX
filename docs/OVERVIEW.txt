ARCHYTASZX DOCUMENTATION
========================

Overview

Applies to: ArchytasZX 2.0.x


1. SCOPE
--------

ArchytasZX is a symbolic engine for proving identities of quantum algorithms whose size is not
fixed. It works on qufinite ZXW diagrams in which the number of qudits, their dimension, and
the number of repetitions of a sub-circuit may all be symbols. Every result the engine reports,
whether a rewritten diagram, a decision of equality or a proof of an identity, holds for every
value of those symbols.

The engine is written in Python (numpy and sympy are its only runtime dependencies) and ships
a Lean 4 library against which its proofs can be re-checked independently.


2. ARCHITECTURE
---------------

The package archytaszx is organised in four engine layers and one front-end layer. Each layer
depends only on the layers below it.

  Layer  Package               Responsibility
  -----  --------------------  -------------------------------------------------------------
  A      archytaszx.algebra    Dimension expressions, phases, exact scalars.
  B      archytaszx.diagram    Generators, diagrams, bang boxes, scalable notation,
                               validation, structural comparison.
  C      archytaszx.rewrite    Rules, matching, the rewrite engine, strategies, normal form,
                               equality saturation, tactics, caching.
  D      archytaszx.semantics  Denotation, contraction, the oracle, deciding equality, proof
                               search, induction, certificates, proof kernel, Lean export.
  -      archytaszx.repl       The diagram parser and printer, ZX drawing, the engine
                               commands, the shell.


3. THE ENGINE
-------------

The engine commands (ENGINE_COMMANDS.txt) operate on qufinite ZXW diagrams: graphs of Z and X
spiders, Fourier boxes, triangles, W nodes and dimension connectives, in which every port
carries its own dimension and bang boxes stand for a symbolic number of copies or legs. On such
diagrams the engine applies rewrite rules (RULES.txt), reduces to a normal form, saturates an
e-graph, searches for proofs, proves families by induction over multiplicities, issues
replayable certificates, re-checks proofs in a small kernel and exports them to Lean 4
(PROOFS.txt, LEAN.txt). The commands are run from the archytaszx shell (SHELL.txt); every
layer is also usable from Python (PYTHON_API.txt).


4. DESIGN PRINCIPLES
--------------------

4.1 Sizes are symbols

A dimension is a Dim (ALGEBRA.txt): a positive integer, a symbol, or a product or power of
these. A multiplicity is a Mult (DIAGRAM.txt). A concrete value typed by the user is abstracted
into a fresh symbol on entry, with the value recorded in the diagram's parameter environment
and substituted back only when output requires it. Representing |k>^30 therefore costs one
leg under a bang box, not thirty legs.

4.2 Scalars are exact

Every scalar is an exact expression over rationals, roots of unity, dimension powers and free
symbols. No module provides any way to drop a global factor or to normalise to a leading
coefficient. Rewrite rules that change the scalar record the exact factor they introduce, so a
derivation proves an equality, not a proportionality. Comparison up to a global phase exists
only as an explicit opt-in mode of the numeric oracle.

4.3 Results are derived, not sampled

Results are obtained by symbolic rewriting and symbolic contraction. Numeric evaluation exists
in the engine only as an oracle: it evaluates small concrete instances to look for counterexamples and to cross-check
derivations, and its findings are reported as such.

4.4 Sound, not complete

Deciding equality of diagrams is undecidable in the generality the engine accepts (symbolic
dimensions, symbolic phases, sums). Every decision procedure is therefore sound but
incomplete: "equal" is reported only with a proof, "unequal" only with a counterexample, and
everything else is "unknown". The same holds for scalars and phases, whose equality tests
report "not equal" whenever equality cannot be verified.

The rule system itself is complete where [P1] and [P2] are: for diagrams without bang boxes at
concrete dimensions, any two that denote the same map are joined by a derivation from the
registered rules (COMPLETENESS.txt). That is derivability, not decision: the decision
procedures above stay sound and incomplete, and the exhaustive mode of prove searches for
such derivations without bound.

4.5 Every proof can be re-checked

A derivation is recorded step by step with full provenance. A certificate replays it from the
recorded steps; the proof kernel rebuilds it from a small set of inference rules; the Lean
export restates it over a tensor-network semantics proved in Lean and Mathlib. Assumptions a
derivation relied on (dimension equalities it could not decide) are carried into the
certificate and into the Lean theorem as hypotheses.


5. WHAT THE ENGINE GUARANTEES
-----------------------------

  - A rewrite never mutates its input diagram, and never produces a diagram with a hard
    validation error that its input did not have.
  - A rule fires only when every one of its declared side conditions has been checked and
    passed; the outcome of each is recorded.
  - A reported EQUAL verdict rests on a replayable derivation, a symbolic contraction with
    every symbol formal, or an induction whose base and step are both settled.
  - A reported UNEQUAL verdict rests on an interface mismatch, a numeric counterexample at an
    assignment satisfying every symbol's assumptions, or a symbolic witness entry.
  - A proof exported to Lean is checked by Lean independently of the engine. A step for which
    no Lean lemma exists appears as an explicitly named axiom (unproved_*), never silently.


6. CURRENT LIMITS
-----------------

  - Decision procedures can return UNKNOWN; read the reason they give.
  - Symbolic contraction becomes slow as the number of phased spiders grows (roughly a factor
    of four per symbolic phase in a chain).
  - Symbolic contraction refuses a bang box wired to a node outside it, and a port box over two
    W nodes.
  - The dimension unifier defers equations with several solution branches, such as
    d1*d2 == 6 or d^n == d^m; such equalities become recorded assumptions.
  - A rule restricted to prime dimensions does not fire at a symbolic dimension.
  - Some proof steps have no Lean lemma yet; they are listed in LEAN_GAPS.txt. This includes
    every rule of the complete axiomatisation (COMPLETENESS.txt) and every spider with
    complex weights.
  - The completeness of the rule system holds at concrete dimensions without bang boxes; the
    exhaustive search may not return on inputs it cannot join.
  - The Python API is not yet stable between releases.
